# MediPredict AI

## Medical E-Commerce & Insurance Intelligence Platform

MediPredict AI is an AI/ML-based platform that applies Machine Learning
and Generative AI techniques to medical e-commerce and insurance-related
business problems.

## Project Objectives

- Medical product demand prediction
- Insurance claim risk prediction
- Customer segmentation
- Medical product recommendation
- LLM-based AI assistant
- Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)
- MCP-based business tools
- AI-driven business insights
